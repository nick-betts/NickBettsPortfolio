03 · Website Redesign

IMLeagues

Redesigning the current IMLeagues website to improve intramural sports experience.

Website UX · Information Architecture · Redesign

Overview

This redesign focused on improving the intramural sports experience by making the website easier to browse, understand, and act within, especially for students looking for schedules, registrations, and team information.

Problem

Existing navigation patterns and page structures made the experience feel more administrative than user-friendly. The challenge was to reduce friction without losing the density of information users need.

Approach

  • Reworked the hierarchy around the most common student tasks.
  • Made schedules, registration, and discovery easier to scan.
  • Used clearer grouping and stronger calls to action across the site.

Outcome

The redesign concept positions IMLeagues as a more approachable and useful product, helping users spend less time decoding the interface and more time participating.
